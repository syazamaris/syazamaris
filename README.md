### What I've done in client work
For a year I was a data analyst at a UK finance and growth consultancy, working on about 14 direct-to-consumer brands in supplements, food, skincare and pet care. The client data is confidential, so here is the work in outline:

- **Ad spend vs customer value:** Linked 58K Meta-acquired customers to the creative angle that brought them in and compared 12-month LTV with CAC across 10 angles. Overall LTV:CAC came out at 0.79, which informed moving budget toward the most efficient angles.
- **Geo-holdout tests:** Designed and ran 12-week geo-holdout tests for YouTube, using difference-in-differences, and tracked them through to a decision. YouTube stayed for one brand where it showed lift, and was cut for another where it made no significant difference. A call-centre reactivation holdout also showed no uplift, so that spend stopped.
- **Failed billing:** Found 2.5K+ subscriptions silently failing to bill during a subscription platform migration, then built a weekly Python scan to catch it happening again.
- **Save-rate audit:** Rebuilt a subscription save rate from raw action logs: 5.7% real, against 12.4% reported. 69% of recorded saves were cancelled within five minutes, which pointed to a problem in the cancel flow rather than a weak offer.
- **Reporting and automation:** Wrote 90+ BigQuery queries and Python automations that turned about two days of manual weekly reporting into minutes, and built the daily-refreshing Data Studio dashboards that founders and growth managers worked from.
